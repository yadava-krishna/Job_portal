# JobPilot — IT Industry & Government Benefits Catalogue

**Document Status:** Official Government Policy & Sectoral Incentive Catalogue  
**Focus:** Financial Assistance, Grants, Subsidies, Export Benefits, Infrastructure & Skilling for the Technology Sector  
**Priority Order:** 1. Karnataka State Policies, 2. Central Government Schemes, 3. Telangana, 4. Tamil Nadu & Other Major IT Hubs  
**Crucial Distinction:** This is **NOT** a government job vacancy portal. This module tracks financial, tax, operational, and development incentives offered by the government to support IT businesses, startups, exporters, and individual technology professionals.

---

## 1. Beneficiary Classification Framework

To prevent misleading claims, every government programme is strictly categorized by its legal beneficiary type:
- **[INDIVIDUAL] Individual IT Professional / Student:** Personal upskilling subsidies, certification fee reimbursements, research fellowships, and digital apprenticeships.
- **[STARTUP] Early-Stage Technology Startup:** Pre-seed and seed stage grants, incubation facilities, prototyping funds, and proof-of-concept capital.
- **[DPIIT_STARTUP] DPIIT-Recognized Startup:** Formally recognized by DPIIT under the Startup India program (eligible for income tax holidays, angel tax exemptions, and fast-track patents).
- **[MSME_TECH] MSME Technology Enterprise:** Micro, Small & Medium Enterprises holding an active Udyam Registration in IT, ITES, software development, or hardware design.
- **[SOFTWARE_EXPORTER] Software & Digital Services Exporter:** Registered entities exporting software, SaaS, or IT-enabled services internationally (eligible for STPI benefits and foreign trade incentives).
- **[ENTERPRISE_IT] Established IT / ITES Corporate:** Large technology employers establishing Global Capability Centers (GCCs), semiconductor fabs, or electronics manufacturing clusters.

## 2. Priority 1: Karnataka State Technology Programmes (KITS, KDEM & Startup Karnataka)

Karnataka is India's primary technology and software capital. The following schemes are active under the **Karnataka IT Policy (2020–2025)** and the **Karnataka Startup Policy**.

### KRN-01: ELEVATE Karnataka (Idea2PoC Grant-in-Aid)
- **Administering Authority:** Karnataka Innovation & Technology Society (KITS), Department of Electronics, IT, Bt and S&T.
- **Beneficiary Category:** `[STARTUP]` Registered technology startups headquartered in Karnataka.
- **Financial Benefit:** **Grant-in-Aid up to ₹50 Lakhs (non-dilutive, zero equity)** distributed across milestone-linked tranches.
- **Core Eligibility Requirements:**
  - Must be a registered entity (Pvt Ltd / LLP / Partnership) incorporated in Karnataka for $\le 10$ years.
  - Startup must have an innovative technology product or digital solution with intellectual property.
  - At least 50% of the founding team must be residents of Karnataka.
- **Application Process:** Annual call for proposals published on the official Startup Karnataka portal; two-stage pitch evaluation by technical and financial grand juries.
- **Official Policy Document:** Karnataka Startup Policy (G.O. No. ITD 19 PRM 2022).
- **Official Portal:** `https://startup.karnataka.gov.in/elevate/`
- **Status:** **Active** (Verified November 2026).

---

### KRN-02: Lease Rental Subsidies for IT/ITES Beyond Bengaluru
- **Administering Authority:** Karnataka Digital Economy Mission (KDEM) / KITS.
- **Beneficiary Category:** `[MSME_TECH]`, `[SOFTWARE_EXPORTER]` IT and ITES companies establishing operations in Tier 2/3 clusters (Mysuru, Hubballi-Dharwad, Mangaluru, Kalaburagi, Belagavi).
- **Financial Benefit:** **50% reimbursement of lease rental charges up to ₹10 Lakhs per annum** for a period of up to 3 years.
- **Core Eligibility Requirements:**
  - Registered IT/ITES/BPO enterprise operating outside Bengaluru Urban and Rural districts.
  - Minimum of 20 full-time technology professionals employed on local payroll.
  - Valid Udyam Registration or STPI registration.
- **Application Process:** Quarterly reimbursement filings via the single-window KDEM portal with audited rent receipts and PF returns.
- **Official Policy Document:** Karnataka IT Policy 2020-2025 (Beyond Bengaluru Initiative).
- **Official Portal:** `https://kdem.karnataka.gov.in/`
- **Status:** **Active** (Verified November 2026).

---

### KRN-03: Patent Reimbursement Incentive (K-tech)
- **Administering Authority:** Karnataka Innovation & Technology Society (KITS).
- **Beneficiary Category:** `[STARTUP]`, `[MSME_TECH]` Karnataka-registered technology businesses and individual inventors.
- **Financial Benefit:** **100% reimbursement of patent filing costs up to ₹2 Lakhs for domestic (Indian) patents and up to ₹10 Lakhs for international (PCT/USPTO/EPO) patents.**
- **Core Eligibility Requirements:**
  - Startup recognized under Startup Karnataka or MSME holding Udyam Certificate.
  - Patent must be granted or officially published in technology, electronics, biotechnology, or software.
- **Application Process:** Online claim submission along with certified patent grant certificates and fee invoices.
- **Official Portal:** `https://startup.karnataka.gov.in/patent-reimbursement/`
- **Status:** **Active** (Verified November 2026).

---

### KRN-04: Digital Skill Development & Certification Subsidies
- **Administering Authority:** Karnataka Skill Development Corporation (KSDC) & KDEM.
- **Beneficiary Category:** `[INDIVIDUAL]` Engineering graduates, tech job seekers, and IT professionals residing in Karnataka.
- **Financial Benefit:** **Reimbursement of 50% to 100% of certification costs (up to ₹25,000 per candidate)** for certified courses in AI/ML, Cloud Computing, Cybersecurity, and Full Stack Development.
- **Core Eligibility Requirements:**
  - Karnataka domicile (Voter ID or Domicile Certificate).
  - Enrolled in or completed an accredited certification (AWS, Microsoft, Google Cloud, NASSCOM FutureSkills Prime).
- **Official Portal:** `https://skillconnect.ka.gov.in/`
- **Status:** **Active** (Verified November 2026).

## 3. Priority 2: Central Government Technology & Startup Programmes

The Government of India provides extensive statutory, financial, and infrastructural frameworks for the IT and startup sectors.

### CEN-01: Startup India Seed Fund Scheme (SISFS)
- **Administering Authority:** Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT), Ministry of Commerce & Industry.
- **Beneficiary Category:** `[DPIIT_STARTUP]` Early-stage DPIIT-recognized tech startups.
- **Financial Benefit:**
  - **Grants up to ₹20 Lakhs** for proof of concept, prototype development, or product trials.
  - **Debt / Convertible Debentures up to ₹50 Lakhs** for market entry, commercialization, and scaling.
- **Core Eligibility Requirements:**
  - DPIIT recognition number required.
  - Incorporated not more than 2 years ago at the time of application.
  - Must not have received more than ₹10 Lakhs of monetary support under any other Central/State scheme.
- **Official Policy Document:** SISFS Operational Guidelines (DPIIT F.No. 5(1)/2020-Startup India).
- **Official Portal:** `https://seedfund.startupindia.gov.in/`
- **Status:** **Active** (Verified November 2026).

---

### CEN-02: Income Tax Exemption under Section 80-IAC
- **Administering Authority:** Inter-Ministerial Board (IMB), Central Board of Direct Taxes (CBDT) & DPIIT.
- **Beneficiary Category:** `[DPIIT_STARTUP]` Technology startups with high innovation potential.
- **Financial Benefit:** **100% income tax exemption on profits for 3 consecutive financial years** out of the first 10 years of incorporation.
- **Core Eligibility Requirements:**
  - Incorporated as a Private Limited Company or LLP between April 1, 2016 and March 31, 2025 (extended).
  - Total annual turnover $\le ₹100$ Crores in any previous financial year.
  - Holds certification of eligible business from the Inter-Ministerial Board.
- **Official Portal:** `https://www.startupindia.gov.in/content/sih/en/tax-exemptions.html`
- **Status:** **Active** (Verified November 2026).

---

### CEN-03: STPI Software Technology Parks (STP) Scheme
- **Administering Authority:** Software Technology Parks of India (STPI), Ministry of Electronics and IT (MeitY).
- **Beneficiary Category:** `[SOFTWARE_EXPORTER]` IT/ITES software exporters and SaaS businesses.
- **Financial Benefit:**
  - **100% Customs Duty Exemption** on imported capital goods, servers, and hardware.
  - Full excise duty exemption on indigenous technology procurement.
  - High-speed international data communication infrastructure with bonded warehouse status.
  - Green card status for streamlined clearances.
- **Core Eligibility Requirements:**
  - Entity must be registered as an STP unit with STPI.
  - Committed to export of computer software and IT services with positive Net Foreign Exchange (NFE).
- **Official Portal:** `https://www.stpi.in/en/software-technology-park-stp-scheme`
- **Status:** **Active** (Verified November 2026).

---

### CEN-04: Credit Guarantee Scheme for Startups (CGSS) & MSMEs (CGTMSE)
- **Administering Authority:** National Credit Guarantee Trustee Company (NCGTC) / Ministry of MSME.
- **Beneficiary Category:** `[DPIIT_STARTUP]`, `[MSME_TECH]` Technology businesses seeking working capital or scaleup debt.
- **Financial Benefit:** **Collateral-free loans up to ₹10 Crores (CGSS) and up to ₹5 Crores (CGTMSE)** with government-backed credit guarantee coverage of 75% to 85%.
- **Core Eligibility Requirements:**
  - Startups must hold DPIIT recognition; MSMEs must possess active Udyam Registration.
  - Loans disbursed through scheduled commercial banks and approved venture debt funds.
- **Official Portal:** `https://www.cgtmse.in/` and `https://www.ncgtc.in/`
- **Status:** **Active** (Verified November 2026).

---

### CEN-05: FutureSkills Prime (MeitY & NASSCOM Upskilling Ecosystem)
- **Administering Authority:** Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) & NASSCOM.
- **Beneficiary Category:** `[INDIVIDUAL]` Technology job seekers, fresh engineers, and working IT professionals.
- **Financial Benefit:** **Direct government cash incentives up to ₹14,500** directly credited via DBT upon successful completion and assessment in emerging technology pathways (AI, Cloud, Big Data, Cyber Security, IoT).
- **Core Eligibility Requirements:**
  - Indian citizen with valid Aadhaar authentication.
  - Completion of accredited National Occupational Standards (NOS) certified exam.
- **Official Portal:** `https://futureskillsprime.in/`
- **Status:** **Active** (Verified November 2026).

## 4. Priority 3: Telangana State Programmes (ITE&C Department & T-Hub)

Telangana is a major IT/ITES innovation hub with tailored support through the **Telangana IT Policy (2021–2026)**.

### TEL-01: T-Fund (Telangana State Innovation Seed Fund)
- **Administering Authority:** Information Technology, Electronics & Communications (ITE&C) Department & T-Hub.
- **Beneficiary Category:** `[STARTUP]` Startups registered in Telangana with operational facilities in Hyderabad or Tier 2 tech hubs (Warangal, Karimnagar).
- **Financial Benefit:** **Seed equity funding and convertible notes between ₹25 Lakhs and ₹1 Crore** per eligible startup.
- **Core Eligibility Requirements:**
  - Entity registered in Telangana; working prototype or proof-of-concept in IT/AI/SaaS/Fintech.
- **Official Portal:** `https://t-hub.co/t-fund/`
- **Status:** **Active** (Verified November 2026).

---

### TEL-02: Telangana IT/ITES Fiscal Incentives (GRID Policy)
- **Administering Authority:** ITE&C Department, Government of Telangana.
- **Beneficiary Category:** `[MSME_TECH]`, `[ENTERPRISE_IT]` IT/ITES enterprises expanding in non-traditional IT zones (GRID Policy — Growth in Dispersion).
- **Financial Benefit:**
  - **Power Tariff Subsidy:** ₹2 per unit rebate on industrial electricity for 5 years.
  - **Stamp Duty Exemption:** 100% waiver of stamp duty and transfer duty on office land/lease deeds.
  - **Recruitment Assistance:** ₹10,000 per employee incentive for recruiting local IT talent.
- **Official Portal:** `https://it.telangana.gov.in/`
- **Status:** **Active** (Verified November 2026).

---

## 5. Priority 4: Tamil Nadu & Other Major Technology States

### TN-01: TANSEED (Tamil Nadu Startup Seed Grant Fund)
- **Administering Authority:** StartupTN (Tamil Nadu Startup and Innovation Mission).
- **Beneficiary Category:** `[STARTUP]` Early-stage tech startups registered in Tamil Nadu.
- **Financial Benefit:** **₹15 Lakhs equity-free grant** disbursed in three tranches.
- **Core Eligibility Requirements:**
  - Tamil Nadu registered entity with DPIIT or StartupTN registration.
- **Official Portal:** `https://startuptn.in/tanseed`
- **Status:** **Active** (Verified November 2026).

---

### MAH-01: Maharashtra IT/ITES Policy Incentives
- **Administering Authority:** Directorate of Industries, Government of Maharashtra.
- **Beneficiary Category:** `[MSME_TECH]`, `[SOFTWARE_EXPORTER]` Tech enterprises in Pune, Navi Mumbai, and Tier 2 hubs.
- **Financial Benefit:** 100% stamp duty exemption on leases, electricity duty exemption for 10 to 15 years, and patent filing subsidies up to ₹5 Lakhs.
- **Official Portal:** `https://di.maharashtra.gov.in/`
- **Status:** **Active** (Verified November 2026).

### 6.1 Database Schema (`government_benefits` module)

```sql
CREATE TABLE it_government_schemes (
    id SERIAL PRIMARY KEY,
    scheme_code VARCHAR(30) UNIQUE NOT NULL, -- KRN-01, CEN-01, TEL-01
    name VARCHAR(255) NOT NULL,
    governing_body VARCHAR(200) NOT NULL, -- KITS, DPIIT, MeitY, STPI
    jurisdiction VARCHAR(50) NOT NULL, -- Karnataka, Central, Telangana, Tamil Nadu
    beneficiary_type VARCHAR(50) NOT NULL, -- INDIVIDUAL, STARTUP, DPIIT_STARTUP, MSME_TECH, SOFTWARE_EXPORTER
    benefit_type VARCHAR(50) NOT NULL, -- GRANT, SUBSIDY, TAX_EXEMPTION, LOAN_GUARANTEE, SKILLING
    max_amount_inr NUMERIC(14, 2), -- 5000000.00 for ₹50L
    eligibility_criteria JSONB NOT NULL,
    application_process TEXT NOT NULL,
    official_portal_url VARCHAR(500) NOT NULL,
    policy_doc_reference VARCHAR(300),
    is_active BOOLEAN NOT NULL DEFAULT TRUE,
    last_verified_at TIMESTAMPTZ NOT NULL DEFAULT NOW()
);
```
